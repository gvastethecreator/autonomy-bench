A small experiment in emergence

Nobody is in charge.
Everybody plays a part.

Each ant wanders in search of food. When it finds some, it carries a piece home and leaves a pheromone trail. Other ants sense that trail and are more likely to follow it.

Trails fade over time. Successful routes get reinforced. Out of these simple rules, a whole colony finds its way.

Try it: Add food far from the nest, place a rock in a busy route, or turn up exploration. Watch the colony adapt.
