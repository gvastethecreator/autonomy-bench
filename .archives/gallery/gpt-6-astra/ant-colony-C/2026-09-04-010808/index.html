NOTES FROM THE FOREST FLOOR

A collective kind of clever.

There is no master plan down here. A colony emerges from simple choices, made together, again and again.

Wander. Discover. Return.

Workers explore the forest floor for food. The little green speck on an ant means it's carrying a meal home. Click a worker to follow its journey.

Leave a little direction.

Returning ants leave pheromones. Others sense these traces and follow. Adjust trail persistence to see paths fade quickly or grow stronger.

Make a small change.

Add a food source or place a stone in their path. Give the colony a moment to discover your change, then watch a new route take shape.
