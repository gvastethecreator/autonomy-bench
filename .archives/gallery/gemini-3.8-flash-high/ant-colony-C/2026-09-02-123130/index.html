Myrmecia Terrarium Guide

Myrmecia is an emergent, biophysically inspired ant colony simulation driven by stigmergy — indirect coordination through self-reinforcing chemical pheromone fields.

Pheromone Communication

  • Cyan (Home Trail): Deposited continuously by ants as they search. Allows foragers to navigate back to the nest mound.
  • Amber (Food Trail): Deposited by ants that have grabbed food. Followed by nestmates to establish optimal transport highways.
  • Crimson (Alarm Scent): Released in distress or combat. Summons armored soldier ants and repels timid foragers.

Interactive Tools & Keyboard Shortcuts

  • 1 Inspect: Click any ant to follow its sensory inputs and telemetry.
  • 2 Feed: Drop sugar crystals, sweet nectar dew, oak leaves, or dead beetles.
  • 3 Barrier: Draw stone obstacles and twigs. Watch ants solve complex routing.
  • 4 Scent Brush: Paint custom pheromone gradients directly onto the forest floor.
  • 5 Predator: Spawn a hunting wolf spider. Soldiers will coordinate to neutralize it.
  • 6 Rain [R]: Trigger a refreshing canopy rainstorm that creates water puddles and washes scents.
  • Space Pause / Resume: Freeze time for micro-observation.
  • V View Mode: Toggle Natural Macro, Bioluminescent UV Night, and Lab Pheromone heatmap.
  • M Audio: Toggle procedural binaural nature sounds and stridulations.
  • Scroll / Drag Pan & Zoom: Seamless navigation with inertial camera.
