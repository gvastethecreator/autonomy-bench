The Science of Stigmergy & Ant Colony Optimization

Stigmergy (from Greek stigma "mark/sign" and ergon "work") is a decentralized coordination mechanism where autonomous agents communicate indirectly by modifying their physical environment.

In MYRMECIA, biological ants exhibit emergent collective intelligence through mathematical pheromone reaction-diffusion physics:

  • Home Pheromone (Indigo): Foraging ants leave the nest secreting a fading trail of home scent. Scent intensity degrades logarithmically with distance traveled, giving other ants a compass vector back to safety.
  • Food Pheromone (Emerald): Upon discovering food, ants grasp a morsel, turn 180°, and retrace the home gradient while depositing a potent trail of food attractant.
  • Shortest-Path Convergence: Ants taking shorter paths complete round-trips faster, refreshing the trail more frequently. Evaporation exponentially prunes longer, inefficient detours!
  • Alarm & Soldier Defense (Crimson): When predators attack or drowning occurs, ants release alarm pheromone that scatters workers and summons armored soldier ants to do battle.
  • Queen & Nursery: Gathered food sustains the Queen ant, who continuously lays eggs that develop into larvae and mature into new ants.
