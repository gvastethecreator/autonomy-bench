The Science of Ant Colony Optimization

Stigmergy is an indirect mechanism of coordination where agents leave traces in the physical environment that guide the actions of other agents.

In this simulation, every ant behaves using biologically authentic Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) principles:

  • Home Pheromone (Blue/Purple): Ants emit this as they venture out from the nest. The scent intensity decreases with travel distance, creating an organic homing beacon.
  • Food Pheromone (Emerald/Gold): When an ant discovers food, it takes a chunk, turns around, and follows the Home gradient while depositing strong Food pheromone.
  • Trail Reinforcement: Shorter paths allow ants to complete round-trips faster, depositing more pheromone per minute. Evaporation naturally prunes longer, inefficient detours!
  • Alarm Pheromone (Red): Emitted when spiders attack or hazards occur, attracting soldier ants and scattering workers.
