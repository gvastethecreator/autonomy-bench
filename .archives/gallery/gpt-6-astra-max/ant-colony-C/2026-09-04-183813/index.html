AN EXERCISE IN EMERGENCE
UNDERSTORY / FIELD GUIDE

A trail becomes
a shared idea.

This little ecosystem has no director. Each worker responds to what is nearby, and a colony-wide pattern emerges.

01

Give them something to find.

Choose Add food, then click an open part of the habitat. Scouts explore, find a seed, and carry it home. On a keyboard, press F and then Enter to scatter a patch.

02

Watch information take shape.

Returning ants leave a chemical trail. Switch to Pheromones to see that invisible map. A stronger scent draws more foragers toward a food source.

03

Change one thing. Look again.

Rain slows the ants and washes scent away. Longer scent memory helps trails persist. Adjust the population or the pace to watch the colony adapt.
