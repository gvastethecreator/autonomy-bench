A NOTE FROM THE FIELD

A few simple rules.

There is no master plan here. Each worker responds to what is nearby. Together, their small decisions build a living network.

  1. 01
    Explore the unknown.
    Workers wander until they find seeds or a scent trail. Place food in the meadow to give them something new to discover.
  2. 02
    Leave a little guidance.
    Ants carrying food leave pheromones on the way home. Other ants follow those signals toward food. Open Trail map to see them.
  3. 03
    Adapt, together.
    Scents slowly fade. Empty food patches lose their traffic. Add stones to redirect the ants, or erase patches to change their world.
