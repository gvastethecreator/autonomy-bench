HOW TO PLAY

Objective — Clear each wave. Large asteroids split into medium, medium into small.
Scoring — Large 20 · Medium 50 · Small 100 · Saucer 200–1000. Extra ship every 10,000.
Survive — Wrap-around space. You’re invulnerable for 2 s after spawning / hyperspace. Hyperspace is risky — you can explode on exit.
Tips — Tap fire, don’t spam (4 shots max). Use thrust sparingly — momentum is real. Lure saucers into asteroids.
