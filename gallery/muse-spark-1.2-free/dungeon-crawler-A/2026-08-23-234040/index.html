How the dungeon is built Procedural

Seeded BSP. 7–12 non-overlapping rooms are carved into a 45×35 grid, then linked with L-corridors and 1–2 random loops so every floor is solvable. Generation is deterministic: share a seed and anyone gets the same layout, enemy mix and loot.
  • Fog & FOV — shadow-casting + Bresenham line-of-sight. Explored stays dim, visible is torch-lit.
  • Turn economy — you move, then every visible foe takes one step. Bump to attack, no extra click.
  • Descent — find the cyan ◈ stairs. Each floor adds +1.2 enemies and stronger loot; your stats persist.

Loot & survival Showcase rules

Chests hold weapons (+1…+4 ATK), armor (+1…+3 DEF), gold or potions. Potions heal 14 HP [H]. Level-ups restore half your max HP and raise ATK/DEF.
  • Enemies — Rats, Bats, Slimes, Goblins, Skeletons, Wraiths — each with bespoke HP/ATK. Wraiths phase through sight lines.
  • Sound is synthesized (no assets) — footsteps, clinks, hits and a low dungeon drone when audio is unlocked by a keypress.
  • Tip: use . to wait, let foes step into your blade, and watch the minimap — unlit black is still unknown.
