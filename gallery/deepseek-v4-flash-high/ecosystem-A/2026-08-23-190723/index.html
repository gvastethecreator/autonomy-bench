A self-sustaining ecosystem: plants capture sunlight and draw nutrients from the soil; herbivores eat plants; carnivores hunt herbivores; dead matter decomposes back into soil nutrients. Only sunlight enters — the matter cycle is closed, populations are regulated by food, space and nutrient availability, and every generation inherits slightly mutated traits, so the whole system evolves while it persists.
