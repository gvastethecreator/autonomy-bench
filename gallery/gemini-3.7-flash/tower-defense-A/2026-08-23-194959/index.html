Objective: Protect your Nexus Core from hostile cyber-entities by strategically building defense towers along their movement path.

Controls & Shortcuts:

  • 1 - 6: Select Tower to build
  • Q, W, E, R: Activate Tactical Superweapons (Airstrike, Cryo, Overcharge, EMP Mine)
  • Spacebar: Toggle Pause / Resume
  • F: Cycle Simulation Speed (1x, 2x, 4x)
  • U: Upgrade selected tower
  • S: Sell / Recycle selected tower
  • Esc: Deselect tower / Cancel placement

Tactical Advice:

  • Place Cryo Towers near Plasma Cannons so slow enemies bunch together for massive splash damage.
  • Use Tesla Arcs against shielded disruptors and swarm splitters.
  • Position Support Beacons in clusters of 3+ towers to maximize their fire rate aura.
  • Target priority matters! Set Railguns to "Strongest" to take out Armored Juggernauts.
