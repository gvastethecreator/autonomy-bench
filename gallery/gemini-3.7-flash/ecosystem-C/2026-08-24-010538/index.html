Welcome to Biosphere Aethelia

Aethelia is a fully autonomous 3D living microcosm governed by dynamic Lotka-Volterra trophic balances, spatial foraging, genetic inheritance, and nutrient recycling.

🌱 Autotrophs: Flora and fungi absorb solar photons and soil nutrients. When consumed, they nourish grazers and regrow over time.
🐇 Sylva Hares & 🦌 Aura Stags: Primary consumers that graze, form social herds, flee when predators approach, and reproduce upon reaching energy thresholds.
🐺 Umbra Wolves: Stalk prey in coordinated packs, managing herbivore populations to prevent deforestation.
🦅 Sky Rays: Apex airborne predators gliding across thermal updrafts.
🍄 Decomposers: When organisms die, their essence returns to the soil as glowing spore blooms, replenishing nutrients.
