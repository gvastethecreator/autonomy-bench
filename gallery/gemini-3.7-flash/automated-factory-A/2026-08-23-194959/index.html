Commander's Guide & Hotkeys

🎮 Factory Controls

  • Left Click: Place selected building or inspect existing machine.
  • Click & Drag: Rapidly lay straight and curved conveyor belt tracks!
  • Right Click / Esc: Cancel build mode or deselect machine.
  • Middle Click / Right Click Drag: Orbit & Rotate camera in 3D.
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in and out smoothly.
  • [R] Key: Rotate building orientation (East → South → West → North).
  • [T] Key: Open Tech Tree | [G]: Analytics | [P]: Presets
  • [1] - [9] Keys: Quick select items from the active toolbar dock.

💡 Automation Strategy

1. Extract: Place Ore Extractors on mineral ore patches (Iron, Copper, Coal, Silicon).
2. Smelt & Machine: Route raw ore via Conveyor Belts to Furnaces (makes Ingots) and Stampers (makes Plates, Gears, Wires).
3. Assemble: Feed multiple parts into Assemblers to produce complex Motors, Circuits, and Robotic Arms.
4. Power Network: Place Coal Generators or Solar Panels and connect Power Poles to keep machines energized!
5. Launch & Export: Construct the Space Elevator Launch Complex to launch cargo rockets and fulfill high-value Galactic Contracts!
