Crowd Evacuation Dynamics & Science

This simulator implements the Helbing Social Force Model (SFM) and generalized crowd panic mechanics developed by Prof. Dirk Helbing and colleagues.

⚡ The "Faster-is-Slower" Effect & Arching

When panic increases, individuals push with excessive force against others near exit bottlenecks. This generates physical compression forces, forming rigid circular "arches" or "clogs" across the door. Pushing harder actually reduces the total flow rate!

🏛️ The Pillar / Obstacle Solution

By placing an asymmetric column or barrier upstream of an exit (e.g. in the Helbing Pillar Benchmark preset), incoming crowd momentum is diverted into two laminar streams, absorbing perpendicular lateral forces and dramatically speeding up evacuation!

🚨 Warden & Multi-Exit Routing

In high smoke or panic, regular evacuees suffer from tunnel vision and flock towards the familiar main door. Trained wardens project a guidance field, breaking herd mentality and redirecting crowds toward unused emergency exits.
