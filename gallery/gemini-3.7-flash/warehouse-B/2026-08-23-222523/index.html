🎯 Core Objective

Orchestrate and optimize package flow from incoming supplier trucks, through automated barcode scanners and storage racks, to outgoing delivery trucks before manifests expire!

🚚 Inbound Unloading Inbound trucks arrive at West Bays. Workers unload boxes onto automated scanning conveyor lines.
