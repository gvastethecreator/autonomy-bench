❓ AUTOMATION HANDBOOK & CONTROLS

🎮 Factory Controls

  • Left Click: Place building / Select machine
  • Left Drag: Continuous belt line drag placement
  • Right Click / Drag: Orbit 3D camera / Cancel placement
  • Scroll Wheel: Zoom in & out
  • 'R' Key: Rotate building (0°, 90°, 180°, 270°)
  • 'Q' Key: Pipette (clone building under cursor)
  • 'X' or 'Delete': Toggle Demolish tool
  • 'Space': Pause / Resume time
  • '1', '2', '3': Game speeds (1x, 2x, 5x)
  • 'C': Cycle Camera (Orbit / Top-Down / Drone follow)

⚙️ How Automation Works

  1. Resource Extraction: Place Mining Drills on ore deposits (Iron, Copper, Coal, Quartz, Oil).
  2. Logistics: Connect Drills to Smelters and Assemblers using Conveyor Belts and Robotic Inserters.
  3. Inserters: Place Inserters so their arrow points FROM a conveyor/machine TO another conveyor/machine.
  4. Power: Build Wind Turbines, Solar Panels, or Power Plants and connect with Power Poles to keep machines energized.
  5. Research: Feed Circuit Boards & Science Packs into Research Labs to unlock advanced technologies!
  6. Space Elevator: Launch manufactured microprocessors & quantum cores for massive credit payouts!
