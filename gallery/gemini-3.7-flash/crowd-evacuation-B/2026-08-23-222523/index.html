CrowdSafe User Guide & Theory

Understanding panic mechanics, faster-is-slower effects, and egress physics

1. Helbing Social Force Model

Agents move according to psychological driving forces towards exits, combined with physical elastic repulsion, body compression, and tangential sliding friction when contacting other evacuees and walls.

2. The "Faster-is-Slower" Arching Effect

When panic levels rise, individuals push harder towards doorways. Above critical density thresholds, tangential friction locks people into rigid semi-circular arches, choking exit flow. Widening doors or placing columns before exits disrupts arch formation.

3. Fire & Toxic Smoke Dynamics

Fires produce dense smoke and carbon monoxide that diffuses through open spaces. Smoke impairs visibility and drains agent stamina. If visibility drops, agents switch to wall-following or flock with neighbors unless guided by Safety Wardens.

4. Interactive Controls & Sandbox

Use the Tools tab to ignite fires, draw walls, create new exits, or place Safety Guides. Test custom blueprints and review the automated Safety Audit to optimize building codes!
