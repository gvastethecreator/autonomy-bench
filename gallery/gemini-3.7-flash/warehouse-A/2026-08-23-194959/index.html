Apex Logistics Warehouse Simulation

How goods move from Inbound Suppliers to Outbound Fulfillment

🔄 The Logistics Workflow

  1. Inbound Receiving (West Bays): Supplier trucks reverse into loading docks carrying diverse cargo manifests (Standard, Express, Fragile, Crates).
  2. Intake & Barcode Scanning: Workers unload boxes and transport them to the automated laser barcode scanner to determine routing.
  3. Dynamic Sorting & Conveyor: Packages travel along powered conveyor roller loops. Sorter diverter arms route packages to their designated outbound lanes!
  4. Aisle Racks Buffer: When outbound trucks are at capacity, workers stage excess boxes on high-bay pallet racks (3 levels tall).
  5. Outbound Fulfillment (East Bays): Packages are loaded into departing freight trucks assigned to regional routes (Metro, Airport Cargo, Regional Hub). When full, trucks depart with cash payouts!

👷 Workforce & Equipment

  • • Human Workers: Agile handlers in high-vis vests and safety helmets. Excellent for rapid sorting and conveyor feeding.
  • • Forklifts: Fast heavy-lift vehicles capable of hoisting palletized crates and high-tier rack storage.
  • • AGVs (Autonomous Robots): Guided autonomous mobile robots navigating marked floor lanes with LED status rings.
