Controls & Interaction

  • Grab Tool: Left click & drag directly on cloth to stretch, pull, and release it dynamically.
  • Slice Tool: Drag across the screen to slice and sever cloth threads like razor-sharp shears!
  • Pin Tool: Click any part of the cloth to anchor or unpin vertices in 3D space.
  • Wind Gun: Click & hold to fire an ultra high-speed vortex jet right into the fabric.
  • Throw Ball: Click to launch heavy dynamic cannonballs straight at the cloth!
  • Camera Navigation: Right-Click & drag to Orbit, Middle-Click or Shift+Right-Click to Pan, Scroll wheel to Zoom.
  • Hotkeys: 1-5 switch tools • Space trigger gust • R reset • S slow-mo.
