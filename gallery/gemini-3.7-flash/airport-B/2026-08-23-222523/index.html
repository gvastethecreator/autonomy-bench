📖 AIRPORT OPERATIONS GUIDE & CONTROLS

Welcome to AeroOps Pro Simulator

AeroOps Pro simulates an ultra-realistic, high-density international hub airport with dual parallel runways (09L/27R and 09R/27L), complete taxiway network, 3 passenger concourses, cargo logistics ramp, de-icing facility, and full AI ground service vehicle operations.

🎮 Controls & Navigation

  • Pan: Click & drag on the map or use W/A/S/D or arrow keys.
  • Zoom: Mouse scroll wheel or use + / − buttons on the bottom-right.
  • Select Flight: Left-click any aircraft in the air or on the ground to open its telemetry dossier and manual ATC clearance pad.
  • Camera Modes: Switch between Global Radar, Apron View, Tower Vantage, or Chase Camera using the left toolbar.

🛬 Flight Lifecycle & ATC Clearances

  • Arrivals: Inbound flights enter STAR corridors, align with ILS glideslopes, touch down with reverse thrust, and taxi to assigned terminal gates.
  • Turnaround: At the gate, automated or expedited ground crews hook up jet bridges, unload/load bags, refuel, and service catering.
  • Departures: Once turnaround reaches 100%, pushback tugs maneuver the aircraft onto taxiways for runway line-up and takeoff.
  • Manual ATC Overrides: Select any flight to issue immediate commands: Cleared to Land, Go-Around, Hold Position, Approve Pushback, or Cleared for Takeoff!
