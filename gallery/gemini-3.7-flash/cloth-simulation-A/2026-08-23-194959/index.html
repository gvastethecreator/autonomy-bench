Cloth Physics & Architecture

Position-Based Dynamics

Uses time-reversible Verlet Integration with substepping. Spring constraints (Structural, Shear, and Bending) are relaxed via Gauss-Seidel iterations for realistic resistance.

Aerodynamic Lift & Drag

Cloth triangles calculate surface area and surface normals to compute aerodynamic pressure forces dynamically modulated by 3D turbulence noise.

Structural Tearing

Springs break in real time when exceeding critical tensile strain or when intersected by the user's cutting blade. Mesh geometry updates dynamically without stalls.

Procedural Audio Engine

All cloth rustle, tearing rips, and ball impact sounds are generated dynamically using Web Audio API nodes with kinetic energy tracking.
