🌿 Biogenesis Ecosystem Guide

Welcome to Biogenesis, a biologically closed-loop, autonomous multi-trophic ecosystem simulation featuring continuous natural selection, nutrient recycling, and fluid thermodynamics.

Ecosystem Dynamics:

  • Producers (Flora): Photosynthesize sunlight, water, and soil nutrients. Disperse seeds on wind and animal transit.
  • Primary Consumers (Herbivores): Forage on vegetation, drink at rivers, herd together, and flee predators.
  • Secondary / Apex Predators (Carnivores): Hunt herbivores, manage carrying capacity, establish territories.
  • Decomposers (Fungi & Microbes): Break down fallen carcasses and biological waste into mineral soil nutrients, completing the zero-waste loop.
  • Genetics & Speciation: Every newborn inherits DNA traits (Speed, Vision, Size, Camouflage, Bravery) with mutation drift.

Interactive God Controls:

  • Pan & Zoom: Drag with left mouse / WASD / Arrow keys. Mouse wheel to zoom in and out.
  • Inspect: Click any creature to inspect real-time DNA, family lineage, neural desires, or take direct WASD possession.
  • God Brushes: Use left toolbar to spawn plants, animals, water springs, soil fertilizers, or trigger wildfires and meteors!
