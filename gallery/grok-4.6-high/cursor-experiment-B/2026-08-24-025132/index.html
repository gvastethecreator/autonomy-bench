Afterimage

A cursor with mass. The thread is what the type can feel. Click to pin a moment; hold to add weight.

Lead weight · 56-node filament

The hand is already late. What you meant to point at has become a wake.

A cursor is not a place. It is a delay you agree to call now.

Move slowly and the type remembers. Move quickly and it only sees wind.

The last look stays a little longer than the light.

Move. The bead is late on purpose.

Cursor mode

lag 0px · mass 1.0 · 0 pins · attract

How the cursor works

The pointer is intention. The bead is the hand. The filament hangs from the brass spool and only the thread can lift the type.

Move
The bead follows with mass and a little overshoot.
Click or Enter
Pin the filament where the bead is, not where the pointer is.
Hold or Shift
Add mass. The bead lags more; the thread sags.
1 / 2 / 3
Attract, Repel, Trace. Trace inks a wake and leaves the type in place.
Space
Freeze the plate. Press again to thaw.
Backspace
Lift the last pin.
R
Rewind the thread to the spool.
Arrows or WASD
Move without a mouse. Shift still adds mass.
T
Switch theme. Light mode turns the bead into a stain.
Escape
Close this panel, or thaw a freeze.
