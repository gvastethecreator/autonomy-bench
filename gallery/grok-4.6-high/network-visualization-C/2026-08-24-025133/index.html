A site, not a cloud

Keel Skerry is a volcanic plug in the North Minch. West is water. The armored research cable beaches at BM-01. Campus sits on the eastern terrace. Select a station, trace a path, or run the night watch tour.

Simulated motes show typical January flows: hydrophone bursts, HPC checkpointing, and a quiet management plane. This is not a live feed from the island.
