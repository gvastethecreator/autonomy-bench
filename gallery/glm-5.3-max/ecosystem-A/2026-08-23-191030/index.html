    The loop

    Sunlight and rain grow the plants. Grazers mow them down and turn them into offspring. Hunters pursue the weak, the young and the slow. The fallen feed the soil, and crowding dampens births — so the world balances itself. If a species ever collapses, wanderers migrate in from outside. Water can't be crossed by anyone.
