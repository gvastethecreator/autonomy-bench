fragment·lab

      
    

    
A single-file GLSL playground. Type a fragment shader on the left and it renders live on the right. Every frame stays on your machine.


    
Uniforms, always provided

    

      
u_timefloatseconds on the clock, scaled by the speed slider

      
u_resolutionvec2canvas size in pixels

      
u_mousevec2pointer over the canvas, 0–1, origin bottom-left, smoothed

      
u_a · u_b · u_cfloatthe A / B / C sliders under the canvas

    


    
Your shader

    
Write helper functions plus void main() and assign gl_FragColor. The precision header and the uniforms above are prepended automatically — error line numbers already map to your editor. Click any error to jump to its line.


    
Shortcuts

    
    
      
  • Ctrl+Enter run  ·  Space play / pause  ·  Ctrl+/ comment lines  ·  Tab indent
    • 
    


    
The rest

    
Share encodes the whole shader into the URL. PNG grabs the exact frame. The divider resizes the split (double-click resets it). Each preset remembers your edits locally.
