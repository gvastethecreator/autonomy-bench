INBOUND / OUTBOUND FREIGHT SIMULATION · NIGHT SHIFT FEED

THE FLOW

TRUCK IN→STAGING→HAULERS→CONVEYOR→BUFFER→LOADERS→TRUCK OUT

Inbound trucks back into the west dock and unload freight onto staging pads. Haulers feed the conveyor; it carries packages east to the buffer. Loaders match each package to a docked outbound truck by destination tape color and stack it inside the trailer. Full trucks — or trucks that wait too long — roll out and the dispatcher sends a replacement for whatever is queuing.

CONTROLS

  • Drag pan camera · Scroll / pinch zoom · Double-click re-center
  • Click a worker, truck or package to inspect it
  • Space pause · 123 speed · L tags · M sound
  • R rush order — 90 s of doubled demand, crew moves faster
  • H this panel · Esc close / deselect

WATCH FOR

  • Staging pads back up when the buffer jams — unloading pauses until a matching truck is dispatched.
  • Outbound trucks depart partially full if freight never arrives.
  • The AMR shuttle runs the north aisle regardless. It has opinions.
