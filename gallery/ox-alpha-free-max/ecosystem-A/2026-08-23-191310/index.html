Field notes

    One closed loop drives everything: plants → herbivores → predators → remains → soil fertility → plants. Grazing depletes patches faster than they regrow; corpses and droppings return nutrients that make the next generation of growth richer. Traits mutate at birth, so speeds and vision drift under selection over long runs.
