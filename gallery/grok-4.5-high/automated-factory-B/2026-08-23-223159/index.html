Welcome to Forgeflow

Build a self-running production line. Extract ore, refine it, assemble widgets, and sell them — then expand while the floor keeps working.

  • Place a Miner on the darker ore patches.
  • Build the chain: Miner → Furnace → Press → Assembler → Depot.
  • Connect stages with Belts. Click a machine to rotate its output.
  • Sell widgets at the Depot, then complete contracts to unlock Circuit Fab.
