A Rogue Descent

The old mines still breathe. Torches gutter in halls that rearrange themselves each night. Descend, if you dare.

Keyboard: Enter start · Mouse / touch also work on buttons

How to play

Explore procedural halls. Clear foes, gather relics, and find the stairwell before your torch gutters out. Combat is adjacent — bump into an enemy or press Space toward them. Drink potions from your bag (I). Reach deeper floors for greater peril and greater spoils.
