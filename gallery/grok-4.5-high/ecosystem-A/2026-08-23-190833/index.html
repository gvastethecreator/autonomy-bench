How it sustains itself

Plants grow from sunlight and absorb nearby nutrients. Herbivores graze plants and reproduce when well fed. Carnivores hunt herbivores. Deaths return nutrients to the soil, closing the loop. Tune sunlight and chaos to keep a stable cycle — or watch a cascade unfold.
