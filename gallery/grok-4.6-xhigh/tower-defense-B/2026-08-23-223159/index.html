How to Hold the Pass

The pale road is the only way through. Raiders follow it. Your engines cannot stand on it.

  • Watch-Bow: cheap, fast, one target. Your backbone.
  • Cinder Pot: slow arcing fire. Splashes. Weak against Ashwings.
  • Rime Needle: chills a lane. Frozen hosts take more from Cinder Pots.
  • Stormglass: lightning leaps between clustered raiders and bites armor.

Each engine upgrades twice. Targeting can favor the first on the road, the last, the strongest, or the nearest.
