How it hangs

The sheet is a grid of point masses stitched with distance constraints. Pull it, pin it, or cut a seam and the rest of the cloth keeps its weight.

  • Drag to gather a fold. Right-drag orbits the room.
  • Shift-click or the pin tool tacks a point in place.
  • The cut tool slices stitches you drag across.
