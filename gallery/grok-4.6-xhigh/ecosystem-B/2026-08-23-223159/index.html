Field note

How this pond works

This is a sealed glass. Mass does not leave. Sunlight is the only income: plants and phytoplankton lock dissolved nutrients into bodies and breathe oxygen into the water.

Daphnia comb the green water. Minnows hunt daphnia. Shrimp and snails chew waste. Bacteria return nitrogen to the dissolved pool. Night is a debt the day has to repay.

Add a flake and you break the seal — mass rises, and a bloom may follow. Draw the shade and income stops; the pond eats its stores.
