Nightglass

A night kiln table. Every charge carries heat. Molten glass drags and glows. Cold glass shatters on a hard hit. Ice melts toward the kiln. Quicksilver gathers into heavier pools.

Hands

  • Grab — drag a charge; flick to throw it.
  • Pour — tap to drop, hold for a stream.
  • Throw — pull a rubber band, release to fire.
  • Draw — stroke an iron rail. Hold Shift for a straight bar.
  • Pin / Spring — click two charges, or one charge to hang it from the table.
  • Torch / Quench — hold to heat or flash-cool nearby charges.
  • Bellows — hold to blow from the pointer.
  • Erase — remove a charge, rail, pin, or spring.
