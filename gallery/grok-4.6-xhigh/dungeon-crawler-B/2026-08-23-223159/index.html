How the dark works

Walls cut the lantern into shards. What you cannot see can still reach you. Oil keeps the wick. Keys open brass locks. Six depths. The well waits.

  • WASD / stickWalk the stone.
  • Mouse / aimFace a threat, then click or Swing.
  • Space / StepFlare step. Brief invulnerability. Costs wick.
  • EOpen chests, doors, shrines, stairs, the well.
  • QDrink a flask of oil — wick and a little blood.
  • EscHold still.
