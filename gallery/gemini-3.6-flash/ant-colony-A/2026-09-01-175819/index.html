📖 Ant Colony Simulation Guide

Welcome to the Ant Colony emergent ecosystem simulator. Watch artificial life self-organize via decentralized pheromone chemistry!

Biological Mechanisms
  • Food Trails (Cyan): Ants returning with food deposit trails. Foragers sniff left and right antenna gradients to trace paths.
  • Home Trails (Purple): Exploring ants drop home pheromones and use odometry to navigate back when loaded.
  • Alarm Pheromones (Red): Ants emit alarms when attacked by spiders or enemy colonies, rallying soldier defenders.
  • Brood Cycle: Queens lay eggs ➔ Nurses tend Larvae ➔ Pupae metamorphose into Workers/Soldiers.
