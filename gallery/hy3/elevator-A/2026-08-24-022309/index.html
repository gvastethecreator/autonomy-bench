How it works

Each floor shows up/down call lights and the queue size. Passengers spawn at random floors heading to random destinations. A dispatcher assigns every hall call to the elevator that can reach it soonest, then each car runs a LOOK sweep — serve all stops in its travel direction before reversing. Cars board riders going the same way and unload at their floors (capacity 8).
Single-file simulation · no build step · open in any browser.
