About this run

Occupants wander calmly until a hazard ignites. Once aware, each person steers toward the nearest open exit using a social-force model: they keep their desired speed, avoid walls, and push away from neighbors to avoid crowding. The fire spreads through open space and blocks routes, so timing and exit placement decide who escapes.
