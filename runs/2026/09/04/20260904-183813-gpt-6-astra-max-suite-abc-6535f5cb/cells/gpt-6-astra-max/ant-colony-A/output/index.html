NOTES FROM THE FIELD

Scout. Share. Repeat.

01

A little curiosity goes a long way.

Workers explore the habitat. When they find a food patch, they pick up one seed and head back to the nest.

02

A good route is worth sharing.

Returning ants leave a pheromone trail. Other workers sense it nearby and follow it toward food. Busy routes grow stronger; unused trails gradually fade.

03

Change the world. Watch it respond.

Add food to create a new destination, or place rocks to interrupt a route. Adjust the population and trail memory to see how the colony adapts.
