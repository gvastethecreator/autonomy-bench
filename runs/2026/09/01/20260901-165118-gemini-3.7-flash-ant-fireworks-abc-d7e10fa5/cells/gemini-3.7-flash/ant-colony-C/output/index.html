🐜 Formica: Ant Colony Stigmergy Simulation

Welcome to Formica, a comprehensive agent-based biological simulation of an autonomous ant superorganism.

Ants have no central leader giving orders. Instead, complex colony intelligence emerges through stigmergy—depositing and sensing chemical pheromones, reacting to local stimulus, and interacting with their environment.

Colony Castes

  • 👑 Queen: The beating heart of the colony. Lays eggs in the deep royal chamber when nourished with royal jelly.
  • 🌾 Foragers: Explore the surface, follow food scent trails, harvest crystals, and lay intense return trails.
  • ⚔️ Soldiers: Large-mandible defenders that respond instantly to alarm pheromones and battle spiders.
  • 🍼 Nurses: Tend to the brood chambers, feeding larvae and grooming eggs.
  • 🧭 Scouts: Fast agile pathfinders with wide antennae range that pioneer new territories.

Controls & Hotkeys

  • Drag Canvas / WASD: Pan across surface and subterranean chambers.
  • Mouse Wheel / Pinch: Smooth zoom in and out.
  • 1 - 7 Keys: Select tools (Inspect, Food, Dig, Barrier, Alarm, Spider, Water).
  • Space: Pause/Resume simulation.
  • Q / R: Center on Queen / Reset camera view.
  • M: Toggle procedural soundscape.
