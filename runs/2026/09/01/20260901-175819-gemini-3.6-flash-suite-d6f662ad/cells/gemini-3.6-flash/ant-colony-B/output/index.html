Ecosystem & Mechanics

• Surface World (Above Ground): Foragers search for sugar, fruit, and seeds to feed the colony. Leafcutters harvest plants for the fungus garden. Aphids drink sap and produce sweet honeydew droplets when stroked by ants!

• Underground Nest: Dig chambers and tunnels into the soil. Nurse ants move eggs and larvae between chambers to keep them warm and well-fed.

• Pheromone Trails: Ants communicate via scents! Food trails (green) guide workers to sustenance, home trails (blue) lead them safely back, and alarm signals (red) summon soldier majors to crush threats.

Controls & Shortcuts

• WASD / Arrow Keys or Drag Right Mouse: Pan world camera.

• Mouse Wheel / Pinch: Zoom in and out smoothly.

• Left Click / Drag: Use selected God tool (Inspect, Dig, Drop food, Paint pheromones, etc.).

• Spacebar: Pause / Resume simulation.

• 1 / 2 / 5 Keys: Change simulation speed.

• C: Center camera on Queen's Royal Chamber.

• S: Center camera on Surface Foraging Ground.
