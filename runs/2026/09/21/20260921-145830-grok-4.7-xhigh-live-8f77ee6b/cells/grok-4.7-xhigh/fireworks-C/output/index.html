Last Light is a nighttime fireworks program over a working harbor. It plays in five movements: First Match, Correspondence, Willow Weather, The Crown, and Afterglow, then begins again. Tap the open sky to launch a shell. The sound button, or the M key, turns harbor audio on or off.
